# simulating-ocular-tissue-clearing
This GitHub repository is an extension of the research paper "Development of a Mathematical Model for the Simulation of Ex Vivo Ocular Tissue Clearing". The mathematical proofs of the equations derived in this paper are found here. To account for the specific nature of the CUBIC procedure, multiple equations were coupled with previously derived models.

The derivation process for the mathematical models involves coupling existing functions as variables into a new equation, allowing the model to account for how a change in one parameter affects multiple others. To verify if the equation is accurate in concept and in protocol, the units of both sides of the equations were analyzed. Future research may expand beyond these mathematical models, accounting for more details or simplifying the equations. For example, equations concerning the preparation stage of the procedure or finer characteristics of the biological tissue may be included.
